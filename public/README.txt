Your images go directly in this folder (not in files/).

  me.jpg     -> your portrait, shown on the About page. Square works best,
                around 800x800.
  cover.jpg  -> the wide background image behind the welcome banner on the
                home page. Wide and fairly plain works best, around 1920x900.

Both are referenced in data/site.js as photo: "/me.jpg" and cover: "/cover.jpg".
Set either to null there if you do not want it.
